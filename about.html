About Us

Pioneering Sustainable Mining for a Better Future

At Prowell Mining Nig Ltd, we are more than just a mining company—we are a driving force in Nigeria’s mineral resource industry, committed to innovation, sustainability, and economic transformation. As a subsidiary of TENSUN GROUP, our expertise extends beyond mining to include technology, agriculture, and investment, making us a globally connected enterprise with deep local impact.

Director’s
Message

At Prowell Mining Nig Ltd, we believe that mining is not just about extracting resources—it’s about creating opportunities, empowering communities, and shaping a sustainable future. Our vision is to establish Prowell Mining as a leader in Nigeria’s mining sector by integrating cutting-edge technology, responsible business practices, and long-term environmental sustainability.

We are committed to not only unlocking Nigeria’s rich mineral potential but also ensuring that our operations drive economic growth, create jobs, and support local communities. Our approach is simple yet powerful—global expertise, local impact, and a relentless focus on excellence.

I invite investors, partners, and industry stakeholders to join us on this journey as we pave the way for a brighter, more sustainable mining future in Nigeria and beyond.

– Rowell Yang, Founder & CEO, Prowell Mining Nig Ltd

Who is Prowell Mining Nig Ltd?

Prowell Mining Nig Ltd is a leading mining and resource development company, specializing in the exploration and extraction of gold and tantalum. We are a subsidiary of TENSUN GROUP, with operations spanning Nigeria, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and the USA.

What minerals does Prowell Mining focus on?

We primarily focus on gold and tantalum mining, ensuring responsible extraction processes that align with international standards and sustainability practices.

Is Prowell Mining committed to sustainable and ethical mining?

Yes! Sustainability is at the core of our operations. We use eco-friendly mining techniques, responsible resource management, and land rehabilitation programs to minimize environmental impact.

What investment opportunities does Prowell Mining offer?

We welcome investors who are interested in sustainable mining and long-term growth. Our projects provide high-ROI opportunities backed by TENSUN GROUP’s financial strength and global market reach.

How does Prowell Mining contribute to local communities?

We prioritize local employment, skill development, and infrastructure support. Our goal is to empower communities by creating jobs and fostering economic growth in the regions where we operate.

How can I partner with or invest in Prowell Mining?

If you are interested in business partnerships or investment opportunities, please contact our Investor Relations Team.
