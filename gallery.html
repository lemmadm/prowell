Message

At Prowell Mining Nig Ltd, we believe that mining is not just about extracting resources—it’s about creating opportunities, empowering communities, and shaping a sustainable future. Our vision is to establish Prowell Mining as a leader in Nigeria’s mining sector by integrating cutting-edge technology, responsible business practices, and long-term environmental sustainability.

We are committed to not only unlocking Nigeria’s rich mineral potential but also ensuring that our operations drive economic growth, create jobs, and support local communities. Our approach is simple yet powerful—global expertise, local impact, and a relentless focus on excellence.

I invite investors, partners, and industry stakeholders to join us on this journey as we pave the way for a brighter, more sustainable mining future in Nigeria and beyond.

– Rowell Yang, Founder & CEO, Prowell Mining Nig Ltd
