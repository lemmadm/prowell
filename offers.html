Chinese Translator/Interpreter (Site-Based – Kwara State)

Chinese Translator/Interpreter 

Prowell Mining Nigeria Limited is seeking a dedicated Chinese Translator/Interpreter to support effective communication between our Chinese and Nigerian teams on-site. This role is ideal for a male candidate with strong language skills and a willingness to work in a dynamic mining environment.

Employment Type: Full-Time (Site-Based)
Location: Kwara State, Nigeria
Salary Range: ₦300,000 – ₦400,000/month
Benefits: Accommodation & Daily Feeding Provided
Gender Preference: Male

Key Requirements:
* Fluent in Chinese (Mandarin) and English (speaking, reading, and writing)
* Minimum of 2 years’ experience in translation/interpretation, preferably in industrial or construction settings
* Ability to handle technical terminology and site-based interactions
* Strong interpersonal skills and cultural sensitivity
